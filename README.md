## The checkout mistakes that silently kill your cashback

Cashback portals don't advertise this prominently, but the terms are consistent across the industry, and Backcountry has more exclusions than most stores. If any of these apply to your order, the cashback quietly never posts:

- **Using a coupon code the portal didn't list.** This is the big one. Portals state explicitly that unlisted codes can void the cashback entirely — so before you paste that code from a random aggregator, check whether your cashback portal lists the same deal on its own Backcountry page.
- **Buying excluded categories.** Bikes and frames are excluded everywhere, along with most boutique ski and bike brands — Santa Cruz, Juliana, Yeti, Ibis, Evil, Never Summer, Scott, Syncros, and Specialized SKUs show up on multiple portals' exclusion lists. Gift cards never earn cashback either.
- **Having a Summit Club+ membership.** Orders placed by Summit Club+ members earn nothing through third-party portals. You pick one rebate path per order.
- **Shopping in the Backcountry app.** App purchases don't track.
- **Breaking the session.** Switching devices mid-purchase, clicking through to Backcountry from an email or a search ad instead of the portal link, or leaving items in your cart from a previous visit can all break tracking. Start with an empty cart, go through the portal, finish in one sitting.
- **Volume limits.** Most portals cap Backcountry at 3 cashback-eligible orders per calendar month, one order per shopping trip.
- **Resale and drop-shipping.** Determined by Backcountry unilaterally, and not appealable through some portals — RebatesMe, for instance, doesn't process missing-cashback inquiries for this store at all.

If your cashback doesn't appear within about a week (it's usually visible in 1–2 days), file a missing cashback claim early — though note that some portals explicitly don't support claims for Backcountry, which is worth knowing before you choose where to route the order.
